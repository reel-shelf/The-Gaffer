Wonderkid growth

Play them. Young players grow fastest with regular starts. A wonderkid on the bench barely develops; loan him out if you can't start him.
Watch dynamic potential. Good form raises potential and poor form lowers it. Check a player's potential every few months.
Training matters. Use development plans to push the attributes for his best position, and keep sharpness high.
Scout smart. Set your scouts to young players and look for “Exciting Prospect” or “Showing Great Potential”.

Transfers

Buy early, sell high. Sign prospects in season one, before their value climbs, and sell players past their peak while they still have value.
Use sell-on clauses. When you sell a youngster you can't play, add a sell-on clause to profit later.
Mind the wages. The wage budget often runs out before the transfer budget. Swap some transfer money into wages if needed.
